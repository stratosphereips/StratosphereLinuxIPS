How catch-up works

Peers share recent warnings and incident history from this network. Continue catch-up requests another bounded transfer after a timeout or transfer limit. Check again asks a completed peer for updates.

Local summaries are available to share; storing them does not send them. Imported history keeps original confidence, observation times and expiry, separately from current host scores.

How trust is calculated

Trust is sampled every minute while connected. Its history starts when sampling is enabled.

Trust = (0.7 × reliability + (1 − 2 × local maliciousness) × local confidence) / 2 + agreement credit, capped at 0.85. Communication alone contributes at most 0.35. Old local assessments lose confidence over time. Matching local detections and at least two peer IDs earn a small reward once per peer and target IP. “Agreed” counts these targets; TP/FP counts separate validations. Hover over trust for its inputs.
